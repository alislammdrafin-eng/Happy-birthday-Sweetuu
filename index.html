👑 For My Queen DIA

To the girl who makes my world a little more beautiful,

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️

I wish I could put into words just how much you mean to me, but somehow, every word feels too small when it comes to you. How do I describe someone who has become such a beautiful part of my existence? How do I explain the happiness I find in your presence, the peace I feel in your words, and the countless little moments that become special simply because they have you in them?

Today is the day the world was gifted with someone who would eventually become my favourite person. And honestly, I don't think you realise how grateful I am that our paths crossed. Out of all the people in this enormous world, I got to know you, fall for you, and call you mine. If that's not something worth being grateful for, I don't know what is.

I want you to know that I don't just love the beautiful pictures you send me, your adorable little smiles, or the way you make my heart skip a beat. I love the little things too. Your random conversations, your silly complaints, your endless stories, your stubbornness, your childish moments, even those little things you probably don't realise I notice. I love the person you are when nobody is watching. I love every version of you, even the ones you sometimes struggle to love yourself.

And if there's one thing I wish for you today, it's that life treats your heart as gently as it deserves. May you never run out of reasons to smile. May every dream you've quietly kept inside your heart find its way into reality. May you always find warmth on your coldest days, courage in your hardest moments, and happiness in the simplest things. I hope you never have to question your worth, because you deserve so much more beautiful things than this world could ever put into words.

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect, or that we'll never disagree, but I can promise that my love isn't something I want to reserve only for the easy days. I want to be there for the ordinary mornings, the unexpected evenings, the silly arguments, the beautiful milestones, and all those little chapters that make a lifetime. I want to keep discovering new reasons to fall in love with you, even after I've run out of words to explain why I did in the first place.

And someday, when we look back at all these birthdays, I hope we'll have countless beautiful memories to laugh about. I hope we'll remember how young we were, how deeply we loved, and how we kept choosing each other through all the different seasons of life.

If I were granted a thousand wishes today, I'd probably spend most of them wishing for your happiness. And with whatever wishes remained, I'd wish for more time, more memories, more sunsets, more conversations, and more birthdays beside you.

Happy Birthday, my favourite human.

Thank you for existing. Thank you for being you. And thank you for giving my heart someone so wonderful to love.

I hope this year brings you everything your beautiful heart has been waiting for. And I hope, in every little and big way, I get to be a part of that happiness.

I love you more than my words could ever explain, more than these paragraphs could ever express, and perhaps a little more with every passing day.

Here's to you, my love. Today, tomorrow, and all the beautiful tomorrows yet to come. ❤️

Forever Yours, Always
